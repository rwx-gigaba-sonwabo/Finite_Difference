Subject: RE: Riskworx Autocall Validation Update (23/10/2026)

Good day all,

Please find below an update on the autocall validation.

1. Base valuation: largely complete
Using the agreed approach (forward/flat volatility, with each autocall priced as its booked components), we have tested RiskFlow's base valuation across a broad set of autocall trades. Our independent Monte Carlo challenger model shows close alignment, and we are comfortable with the pricing mechanics. The results now need to be written up in the validation document.

The digital skew issue raised last week has been resolved and verified. RiskFlow and the validation model now apply the same method for the volatility skew adjustment on both the digital and the digital contra options.

As further evidence, we will plot the convergence of our full Monte Carlo against RiskFlow's One-Step Survival (OSS) pricing for the base trades. This will show the number of simulations at which the two converge and confirm that the OSS pricing works as expected. It will also support our use of OSS in the future exposure work (see below).

2. Future exposure profiles (PFE): in progress, approximately 60% complete
We expect to complete this by Friday, 30 October 2026.

Pricing the autocalls with a full Monte Carlo inside the outer exposure simulation is not feasible on the available hardware. As OSS computes survival probabilities analytically, it is far more efficient. We will therefore validate RiskFlow's OSS pricer on future exposure dates using our own OSS implementation. To confirm this remains appropriate, we will also test the convergence of our full Monte Carlo against RiskFlow's OSS on a sample of exposure dates. The rationale for this choice will be recorded in the validation document.

3. SIMM sensitivities: planned for 29 October to 6 November 2026
This depends on the SIMM calculation code in RiskFlow being ready. Once available, we will validate the sensitivities and include them in the validation document. If this cannot be completed before the mid-November deadline, it will be carried forward as an agreed post-deadline action item, as discussed in today's meeting.

Priorities, in order:
1) Base valuation
2) Future exposure simulation (PFE)
3) SIMM sensitivities

Market data
- Dividends: confirmed by IPV as available in Front Arena and mapped.
- Correlations: confirmed by IPV as available in Front Arena and mapped.
- Volatility: up-to-date surfaces are currently available only for the S&P 500 and EURO STOXX 50. The business has approved the cost of the remaining data, which is now going through procurement and legal. Once approved, IPV will source quotes from ICE and calibrate SVI surfaces for the other underlyings. We expect these to be mapped in the coming weeks.

The volatility constraint does not affect the validation of RiskFlow's pricing mechanics. It does mean that, for now, comparisons against hedge counterparty valuations are limited to trades on the two indices above. We believe these trades are sufficient to assess the materiality of the forward/flat volatility approach.
