Please find below an update on the autocall validation.

1. Base valuation: largely complete

Using the agreed approach (forward/flat volatility, with each autocall priced as its booked components), we have tested RiskFlow's base valuation across a broad set of autocall trades. Our independent challenger model shows close alignment, and we are comfortable with the general pricing mechanics.

One issue has been identified. It concerns the digital skew adjustment, the setting that accounts for the volatility smile when pricing digital options:

RiskFlow applies the adjustment to the European digital option, but not to the barrier digital option booked to offset it when an autocall is called.
With the adjustment switched on, the European digital's price moves by roughly 25–30%, depending on where spot sits relative to the autocall barrier. The offsetting barrier digital does not move.
With the adjustment switched off, RiskFlow and our challenger align closely.
We are now quantifying the net effect on the overall autocall price. If it is material, a code change will be needed in RiskFlow so that the barrier digital is priced on the same basis.

2. Future exposure profiles (PFE): in progress

Work on pricing the autocalls through future exposure simulation, a prerequisite for the PFE calculations, has begun. We expect to complete it over the next week.

3. SIMM sensitivities: subject to timelines

Once the SIMM calculations in RiskFlow have been updated, we will validate the corresponding sensitivities and include them in the validation document. If this cannot be completed before the mid-November deadline, it will be carried forward as an agreed post-deadline action item, as previously discussed.

Priorities, in order:

Base valuation pricing
Future exposure simulation (PFE)
SIMM sensitivities
4. Market data limitation

Up-to-date volatility surfaces are currently available only for the S&P 500 and EURO STOXX 50. We will therefore compare base valuations against hedge counterparty prices for those trades only.

This does not affect the validation of RiskFlow's pricing mechanics.
It does limit our ability to show the impact of the forward/flat volatility model across the full portfolio.
The remaining volatility data is going through legal and procurement. Once approved, IPV will source quotes from ICE and calibrate SVI surfaces for the other underlyings.
