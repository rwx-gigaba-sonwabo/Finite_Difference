Subject: RE: Riskworx Autocall Validation Update (30/10/2026)

Good afternoon all,

Please find below an update on the autocall validation.

Summary
Base valuation and future exposure testing are complete, and both are reflected in the validation document. SIMM sensitivities have been put on hold until after the MRF. Excluding SIMM, the validation is approximately 80% complete, and we remain on track for the mid-November deadline.

1. Base valuation: Completed
- Using the agreed approach (forward/flat volatility, with each autocall priced as its booked components), we tested RiskFlow's base valuation across a broad set of autocall trades. Our independent Monte Carlo model aligns closely with RiskFlow, with relative differences well below 1%. We are comfortable with the pricing mechanics.
- We also compared our full-path Monte Carlo with RiskFlow's One-Step Survival (OSS) pricing. RiskFlow's OSS pricer converges to the same value as our model, but with far fewer simulations (approximately 1,000 against 500,000). This confirms both the accuracy and the efficiency of RiskFlow's OSS implementation. On this basis, 500,000 simulations were used for all base valuation comparisons.

2. Future exposure profiles (PFE/CVA): Completed
- Exposure simulation requires the trade to be repriced on every future date of every market scenario (5,000 to 10,000 simulations on both the outer and inner paths). With full-path Monte Carlo, this proved too computationally intensive for the available hardware, with very long run times per trade.
- As OSS calculates survival probabilities analytically, it is far more efficient, and the convergence analysis above supports its use. We therefore validated RiskFlow's OSS pricer on future exposure dates using our own OSS implementation.
- The results show close alignment between the validation model and RiskFlow (relative differences below 1%). This confirms the accuracy of RiskFlow's OSS implementation for pricing autocalls in exposure simulation.

3. SIMM sensitivities: On hold until after the MRF
- On 29 October 2026 we were advised that the SIMM calculation code in RiskFlow is ready. However, no SIMM implementation or development documentation is available to us, and this was stated as a prerequisite for the validation.
- SIMM has also not previously been validated in RiskFlow. As discussed in today's meeting, an internal discussion between Investec and Riskworx is likely needed on what an inception SIMM validation would require.
- With 6 to 7 business days remaining before the target pre-MRF date (approximately Thursday, 12 November 2026), the SIMM component has been put on hold. It will be addressed after the proposed MRF date (approximately 16 November 2026).
- The validation document presented at the MRF will therefore cover base valuation and future exposure results only (points 1 and 2 above).

Market data (unchanged from the update of 16 October 2026)
- Dividends and correlations: up to date in Front Arena and mapped.
- Volatility: up-to-date surfaces are currently available only for the S&P 500 and EURO STOXX 50. The business has approved the cost of the remaining data, which is going through procurement and legal. Once approved, IPV will source quotes from ICE and calibrate SVI surfaces for the other underlyings.

This does not affect the validation of RiskFlow's pricing mechanics. It does limit comparisons against hedge counterparty valuations to trades on the two indices above. We believe these trades are sufficient to assess the materiality of the forward/flat volatility approach.
